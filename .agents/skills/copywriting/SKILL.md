# Copywriting

- Identify the audience, product value, desired action, and evidence available. Do not invent claims, metrics, or guarantees.
- Lead with the reader's problem and a specific benefit. Use concrete language and remove filler.
- Preserve legal, brand, and product constraints supplied by the user. If a claim depends on missing evidence, phrase it narrowly or ask.
- Offer variants only when they help compare tone or positioning. Keep the final copy ready to use.
